The real problem

Taste and clarity beat custom code when the goal is getting interviews.

Do this next

  1. Pick a premium template and customize copy, photos, and projects first
  2. Ship a clean v1 in a weekend; learn advanced tools later if you still need them

Tools vs presentation

Free builders are fine for drafts. When the link goes on a CV, presentation quality becomes part of your professional signal — templates exist so you skip reinventing layout.
