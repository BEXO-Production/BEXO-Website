Design for the skim

Assume ten seconds. If education, skills, and contact aren’t obvious, a busy recruiter won’t hunt. Pair a designed home with a Hire Me page that prints cleanly.

Taste without theatrics

Premium feels quiet: hierarchy, spacing, and one confident accent. If motion or dark themes fight readability, they cost you interviews.
