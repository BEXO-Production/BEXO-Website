Unsplash — night workspace

You don’t need a louder personal brand. You need a clearer one — and a place online that proves it.

The default path (Drive folders, Canva PDFs, unfinished Notion pages) makes you look busy — not hire-ready.

This guide is for anyone whose portfolio will be judged by its words who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

Uniqueness comes from your projects and voice. The platform’s job is to get out of the way and make that voice look premium.

Do this next

  1. Screenshot your homepage and mark the first thing the eye hits
  2. Move proof higher; move biography lower if needed
  3. Publish the change even if it’s imperfect

Share like a professional

Put the link in applications, mentor emails, and community intros. A portfolio that nobody visits is still a draft.

How a website helps you stand out

Clear writing is a career skill. Your site is the public practice ground.

On BEXO, that path is simple: discover on mybexo.com, create on dash.mybexo.com, share on yourname.atbexo.com. Premium templates and a Hire Me page give you agency-grade presentation without becoming a full-time webmaster.

Field checklist

  • Cut adjectives; keep verbs and results
  • Read every bio line out loud
  • Replace buzzwords with specifics

Try this today

Open your draft (or start one), apply one change from the list above, and publish. Momentum compounds faster than waiting for “ready.”
