You don’t need a louder personal brand. You need a clearer one — and a place online that proves it.

This guide is for builders polishing the details that change first impressions who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

Uniqueness comes from your projects and voice. The platform’s job is to get out of the way and make that voice look premium.

Do this next

  1. Delete one section that doesn’t earn its space
  2. Replace one buzzword with a concrete outcome
  3. Ask a friend: “What do you think I do?” — then align the headline

Share like a professional

Put the link in applications, mentor emails, and community intros. A portfolio that nobody visits is still a draft.

Field checklist

  • Delete anything you can’t defend in an interview
  • Test on a phone before you share
