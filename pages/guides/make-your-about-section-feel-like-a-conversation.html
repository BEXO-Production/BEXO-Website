You don’t need a louder personal brand. You need a clearer one — and a place online that proves it.

Recruiters and clients don’t owe you a deep read. If the first screen is confusing, they leave.

This guide is for anyone whose portfolio will be judged by its words who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

A website helps you stand out because it compresses your story into something shareable, memorable, and updatable — qualities a static attachment can’t match.

Do this next

  1. Cut adjectives; keep verbs and results
  2. Read every bio line out loud
  3. Replace buzzwords with specifics

Make uniqueness visible

Your uniqueness is already in your projects and judgment. The website’s job is to frame that judgment so a stranger trusts it quickly.
