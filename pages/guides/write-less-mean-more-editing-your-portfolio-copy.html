You don’t need a louder personal brand. You need a clearer one — and a place online that proves it.

When everyone lists the same tools and clubs, the differentiator becomes how cleanly you present proof.

This guide is for anyone whose portfolio will be judged by its words who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

Clear writing is a career skill. Your site is the public practice ground.

Do this next

  1. Screenshot your homepage and mark the first thing the eye hits
  2. Move proof higher; move biography lower if needed
  3. Publish the change even if it’s imperfect

Edit like a product

Every sentence should earn a click or a reply. Cut throat-clearing openers. Lead with what you make or the problem you solve.
