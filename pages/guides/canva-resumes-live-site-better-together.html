The real problem

Smart portfolios feel calm: one idea per section, outcomes before tools, and a next step that doesn’t beg.

PDF + site, not PDF or site

Keep the PDF for portals that demand uploads. Use the live site for humans. Put the URL in the resume header so both assets point to each other.

Tools vs presentation

Free builders are fine for drafts. When the link goes on a CV, presentation quality becomes part of your professional signal — templates exist so you skip reinventing layout.
