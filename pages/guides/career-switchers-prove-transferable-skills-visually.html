Unsplash — presentation moment

You don’t need a louder personal brand. You need a clearer one — and a place online that proves it.

When everyone lists the same tools and clubs, the differentiator becomes how cleanly you present proof.

This guide is for career switchers and mid-journey professionals who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

Uniqueness comes from your projects and voice. The platform’s job is to get out of the way and make that voice look premium.

Do this next

  2. Move proof higher; move biography lower if needed
  3. Publish the change even if it’s imperfect

Make uniqueness visible

Your uniqueness is already in your projects and judgment. The website’s job is to frame that judgment so a stranger trusts it quickly.
