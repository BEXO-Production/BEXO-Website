Most advice on this topic is vague. This guide is a field note: what to change on your site this week so you stand out for the right reasons.

People delay publishing until it feels perfect. Meanwhile, peers with simpler sites are already circulating a URL.

This guide is for students in active placement or internship season who want a site that feels intentional — not another generic template dump.

The real problem

Smart portfolios feel calm: one idea per section, outcomes before tools, and a next step that doesn’t beg.

Do this next

  1. Delete one section that doesn’t earn its space
  2. Replace one buzzword with a concrete outcome
  3. Ask a friend: “What do you think I do?” — then align the headline

Translate campus work

Replace “CSE Mini Project” with the problem you solved, who it helped, and what changed. Faculty titles impress faculty. Outcomes impress employers.

Time-boxed shipping

Set a timer. Publish with three projects and a clear bio. Perfection is a moving target; a live URL is a fixed asset you can improve.

How a website helps you stand out

When dozens of CVs arrive the same morning, a calm live site is the signal that you finished something and can share it cleanly.

Field checklist

  • URL on resume header and LinkedIn Featured
  • One hero project with outcome metrics
