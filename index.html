Ace Digital · Shore notes

Small studio. Cleaner habits.

We’re not writing a manifesto. We’re a small team shipping student portfolios — and we try not to waste paper, watts, or anyone’s battery.

01 · Placement week

Links over photocopies

Campus drives still mean stacks of printed resumes in plastic sleeves. A live link is one less pile on the desk — and one less trip to the shop.

02 · Older phones

Quiet when it should be

Fancy background video looks great on a new laptop. On a three-year-old phone it just lags. We detect that and show a still instead — same story, smoother scroll.

03 · Near the coast

Care about clean seas

Ace Digital is a small studio that cares about the water we grew up beside. We can’t “offset the ocean,” but we can keep BEXO lean: one API, compressed media, no junk carousels eating data.
