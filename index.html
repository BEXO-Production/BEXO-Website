05 — Studio

Small studio. Cleaner habits.

We're not writing a manifesto. We're a small team building a better way to remember people — and we try not to waste paper, watts, or anyone's battery.

Older phones

Quiet when it should be

Heavy video looks great on a new laptop and lags on a three-year-old phone. We detect that and show a still instead.

Near the coast
